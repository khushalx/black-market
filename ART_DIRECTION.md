# BLACK MARKET — visual direction

BLACK MARKET uses deep charcoal, warm white and a restrained auction-yellow accent. Barlow Condensed gives the wordmark, numbered lots, bids, timers and balances a recognizable typographic identity; Manrope keeps controls and condition notes readable. Both fonts are served locally with their OFL licenses.

The live auction comes first. A large, neutral photo stage sits beside the current price and clock. A split bid control borrows the proportions of a bidding paddle. Numbered bidders share a compact balance strip. Accepted prices and leaders change with short transitions; SOLD appears as plain typography over a faded object. Reduced motion is respected.

On phones, the item, price, clock, bidder balances and fixed bid dock share one view. Secondary actions move into Lot info. Shorter phone viewports use tighter spacing and a smaller photo stage. Lobby readiness and results actions remain reachable at the bottom.

The homepage contains an oversized wordmark, one sentence, real catalog photographs and a clear Play online action. Private room, Practice and code entry remain directly available. Matchmaking replaces the play controls with the actual server wait and Cancel action.

The lobby is an open arrangement of numbered seats, readiness and shared starting cash. Format details expand on demand. Results lead with the winner and final wealth; all six standings remain visible on a normal phone, while receipts, valuation factors and match highlights expand deliberately.

Details, sources, inspections, collection, history, profile and recovery use the same native dialog system. Existing product photographs and credits are retained; homepage photo credits are available under How to play. These are reference photographs, not claims about a physical item for sale.

This redesign changes presentation and interface copy. Auction rules, networking, persistence, matchmaking, catalogs and balances are unchanged. Screenshots of this direction live in `docs/screenshots/art-direction/`; other screenshot folders show earlier designs.
